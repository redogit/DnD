# Research + Production Federation

**Status:** active coordination layer, not a central authority  
**Scope:** ideas surfaced in the current history/playground work plus the related recovered project lineages  
**Rule:** research, production, play, teaching, and history may connect without becoming interchangeable

This document turns the current body of ideas into **workable lanes** without forcing them into one master theory.

The person remains the chooser.

## The five lanes

Every branch may expose any subset of these lanes:

1. **RESEARCH** — ask, model, test, verify, refute, preserve remainder.
2. **PRODUCTION** — build something useful, verify it, ship it within its actual claim boundary.
3. **PLAY** — explore, mutate, combine, create, wander, and make strange things without needing scientific justification.
4. **TEACH** — help a willing learner understand or use something, then release them rather than creating dependency.
5. **HISTORY** — recover predecessors, failures, sources, chronology, and missing context without pretending reconstruction is the original.

No branch is required to use all five.

## Hard boundaries

```text
RESEARCH_RESULT != PRODUCT_FEATURE
PRODUCT_SUCCESS != SCIENTIFIC_VALIDATION
PROTOTYPE != DEPLOYMENT
DEPLOYMENT != HUMAN_BENEFIT
PLAY != EVIDENCE
TEACHING != AUTHORITY_TRANSFER
INDEX != AUTHORITY
RELATION != MERGE
ASSISTANCE != OWNERSHIP
```

A research failure may still inspire a game mechanic or tool. A successful game or app may still teach a useful distinction. Neither transfer changes the evidence status of the source claim.

# The two main loops

## Research loop

`QUESTION → BOUNDARY → CLAIM → TEST → OBSERVATION → COUNTERPROBE → EVIDENCE STATE → REMAINDER → NEXT QUESTION`

Evidence states include proof, exact finite verification, controlled empirical support, negative result, invalid run, hypothesis, historical report, and open/unresolved.

## Production loop

`NEED → USER/CONTEXT → MINIMUM USEFUL BUILD → VERIFY → ACCESSIBILITY/SAFETY/COST CHECK → RELEASE CANDIDATE → SHIP/USE → FEEDBACK → REPAIR/REMOVE/REVISE`

A production loop may stop at prototype, internal tool, local app, public beta, or stable release. Those are different states.

## Bridge rule

The only lawful bridge is explicit:

`research artifact --[typed relation]--> production candidate`

Examples of typed relations:

- `INSPIRES_INTERFACE`
- `PROVIDES_VERIFIED_OPERATOR`
- `SUPPLIES_TEST_METHOD`
- `PROVIDES_COUNTEREXAMPLE`
- `PROVIDES_DATA_FORMAT`
- `PROVIDES_RECOVERY_PATTERN`
- `PROVIDES_GAME_MECHANIC`
- `TEACHING_EXAMPLE_ONLY`
- `HISTORY_ONLY`

No bridge transfers authority by default.

# Current-chat research program

## 1. Epistemology → praxeology → adaptive organization

**Current state:** philosophical/systemic framework; research questions open.

Working distinction:

- epistemology: what is known/warranted;
- axiology: what matters;
- praxeology: what an agent does;
- adaptive organization: how sensing, valuing, acting, correction, and coordination can themselves reorganize while preserving continuity.

Research questions:

- What is the minimum observable structure required to distinguish a static epistemic state from an acting agent?
- Which features of agency survive carrier changes?
- When does a collection of locally acting components warrant treatment as a higher-level coordinated system?
- What invariants preserve identity through reorganization?

Production candidates:

- interactive decision-field visualizer;
- teachable state/action/feedback simulator;
- small agent-society sandbox;
- visual lineage/reorganization explorer.

Claim ceiling:

No universal law of mind, life, society, or civilization is asserted.

## 2. Higher organization

Working lens:

`higher organization = differentiation + relation + coordination`

Research path:

- test whether the relation is descriptive, predictive, or merely useful language in bounded synthetic systems;
- measure where added differentiation helps or hurts collective performance;
- separate coordination from homogenization;
- test failure modes: over-centralization, lost autonomy, hidden authority, brittle interfaces, coordination cost.

Production path:

- federated project/workspace UI;
- detachable typed bridges between projects;
- local authority and evidence badges;
- “split this integration apart” controls;
- visual maps that show shared operations without collapsing identities.

## 3. Cell → animal → technical “we”

Working outward sequence:

`cell → organism → animal/environment → social group → culture → technical extension → distributed human-machine cognition → institutions/civilization → meta-coordination → collective self-modeling`

Research path:

- compare coordination mechanisms across levels without claiming identity;
- identify local boundaries, feedback, repair, memory, and resource flow;
- look for scale-dependent failures where the analogy breaks.

Production path:

- educational comparative simulator;
- systems map with selectable scale;
- “same pattern / different carrier” learning mode;
- accessibility-first explanatory tools.

## 4. Meta-coordination and self-modeling

Research path:

`system → self-model → action → changed system → updated self-model`

Questions:

- When does self-measurement improve coordination?
- When does the self-model become stale or distort behavior?
- Which governance/meta-coordination mechanisms remain reversible?

Production path:

- evidence-aware dashboards;
- reversible configuration/version histories;
- analytics that show observation, interpretation, decision, and revision separately;
- governance interfaces that expose who/what has authority.

## 5. No-center / federated agency

Research proposition:

Centralization can simplify routing while increasing distortion, coupling, authority concentration, and reconstruction cost. Federation may preserve local competence while imposing coordination overhead.

Bounded experiments:

- centralized vs federated task-routing simulations;
- detachable vs irreversible integration tests;
- local terminology preservation vs normalized global schema;
- user recovery burden after integration changes.

Production path:

- `FEDERATED_WORK_INDEX.md`;
- History Playground;
- detachable typed links;
- project-local state and claim labels;
- user-owned navigation rather than a single global rank.

# Promotion gates

Before a research result becomes a production dependency:

1. identify the exact dependency;
2. state whether production needs truth, correctness, performance, or merely inspiration;
3. pin the source artifact/version;
4. identify a fallback if the research interpretation later changes;
5. verify the product independently of the research claim where possible.

Before a production artifact is used as research evidence:

1. preregister the research question when appropriate;
2. identify confounds and selection effects;
3. preserve raw observations separately from product telemetry/summary;
4. keep user behavior/private data out unless explicitly consented and necessary;
5. do not infer scientific validity from adoption, engagement, or revenue.

# Fun remains a production requirement where appropriate

For games, creative tools, educational experiments, and exploratory interfaces:

`FUN` is not scientific evidence, but it is a legitimate design goal.

Useful questions include:

- Did the user choose to continue?
- Could they understand the controls?
- Could they leave without penalty?
- Could they create something they value?
- Could they teach or share what they discovered?
- Did the system preserve their agency and work?

Do not turn every playful interaction into telemetry or optimization pressure.

# Immediate federation priorities

1. **Research switchboard** — make claim/evidence/open-question status browsable without centralizing authority.
2. **Production switchboard** — show apps/tools/games and their actual readiness/verification state.
3. **Teach-back surfaces** — every major public tool should have a “what did you learn / how would you explain it?” optional path.
4. **Accessibility pass** — keyboard, screen-reader structure, reduced motion, forced colors, plain-text alternatives.
5. **Version/restore pass** — every mutable public artifact should preserve a recovery route where practical.
6. **Negative-result gallery** — selected failures that teach why the successor changed.
7. **Fun lane** — games/art/creative tools that are not forced to justify themselves as research.
8. **Research-to-product bridges** — typed, removable, versioned links rather than silent inheritance.
9. **Product-to-research firewall** — adoption/use does not promote claims.
10. **Keep splitting** — if a combined surface becomes cognitively heavy, fork the view before centralizing the underlying projects.

The objective is not to finish everything into one system.

The objective is to make **everything easier to explore, build, test, use, teach, split apart, and revisit**.
