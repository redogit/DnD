# Body of Work — Living Public Map

**Snapshot:** September 14, 2026  
**Status:** bounded recovery map, not a global completeness claim

This document is a public map of the long-running work recoverable from preserved conversations, Library artifacts, research exports, source repositories, and current project records. It is intentionally broader than the profile README.

It does **not** claim that every conversation, idea, theorem attempt, experiment, or artifact from every year has been recovered. Missing material remains missing or unresolved rather than being silently reconstructed as fact.

The main organizing idea currently used across the work is:

> **higher organization = differentiation + relation + coordination**

The associated outward question is how heterogeneous living, human, technical, institutional, informational, and environmental components can coordinate without erasing the distinctions that make them useful.

## Evidence legend

The same word does not mean the same evidence level. This map keeps at least these states separate:

- **PROVED / FORMAL** — supported by a mathematical proof or formal proof artifact within its stated assumptions.
- **EXHAUSTIVELY VERIFIED FINITE** — every case in a declared finite domain was checked.
- **TESTED / RECONSTRUCTED** — supported by executed computation, experiment, or independent reconstruction within a bounded protocol.
- **IMPLEMENTED** — software or machinery exists and has a stated verification boundary.
- **NEGATIVE RESULT** — a proposed mechanism, comparison, or stronger claim failed its declared test.
- **HYPOTHESIS / THEORY** — explanatory or predictive structure that still requires causal or external validation.
- **PHILOSOPHY / ORIENTATION** — a value, lens, or generative viewpoint; never evidence by itself.
- **HISTORICAL / RECOVERED** — preserved from earlier work but not automatically revalidated as current.
- **OPEN / UNRESOLVED** — neither proved nor refuted within the available evidence.

Core boundaries:

```text
USER_INPUT != ASSISTANT_SYNTHESIS
OBSERVATION != COMPARISON != INTERPRETATION
IMPLEMENTED != VERIFIED
REPETITION != INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION
BYTE_IDENTITY != SEMANTIC_TRUTH
FINITE_MODEL != UNIVERSAL_LAW
COORDINATION != HOMOGENIZATION
RECONSTRUCTION != SOURCE
UNKNOWN != FALSE != ABSENT
PROJECT_RELATION != EVIDENCE_TRANSFER
```

## Shared research spine

Across otherwise distinct projects, the most persistent control sequence is:

`Human need / aim → boundary → bounded possibility space → selection → distinction → relation → transform → carrier/action → observation → consequence → evidence-qualified output → unresolved remainder → next bounded state`

Recurring operations are:

1. preserve source, identity, chronology, and provenance;
2. distinguish only differences consequential to the current obligation;
3. transport across carriers without inventing meaning;
4. compose verified operators instead of treating every solution as isolated;
5. attack claims with counterexamples, controls, ablations, and independent reconstruction;
6. select what becomes active only after correctness, relevance, authority, and cost gates;
7. retain the remainder — unknown, absent-from-view, disproved, and superseded are different states.

# I. Foundational / philosophical work

## Adaptive organization and the technical “we”

**Status:** working philosophical/systemic framework; not a universal scientific law.

Current outward sequence:

`cell → organism → animal/environment → social coordination → language/culture → technical extension → distributed human-machine cognition → civilization → planetary coupling → meta-coordination → collective self-modeling`

Working motif:

`SENSE → DISTINGUISH → REMEMBER → MODEL → VALUE → COORDINATE → ACT → REPAIR → LEARN → REORGANIZE`

The central claim is structural rather than identity-based: higher-level capability can arise through coordinated differentiation without requiring the members to become the same thing.

## Epistemology, axiology, and praxeology

**Status:** philosophical/methodological distinction.

- epistemology: what can be known or warranted;
- axiology: what matters or is preferred;
- praxeology: what an agent does under goals and constraints;
- adaptive organization: how the machinery of sensing, valuing, acting, correcting, and coordinating can itself change while preserving enough continuity to remain the same evolving system.

The recurring loop is:

`observe → distinguish → model → value → choose → act → consequence → revise`

## Human direction / Shared Well / Pursuit of Happiness

**Status:** human-purpose constraint and philosophical orientation, not scientific proof.

Repeated themes include human need, minimal necessities, pursuit of happiness, privacy, consent, accessibility, sustainability, opportunity cost, the ability to remain changeable, and doing useful work without denying the same possibility to others.

## Philosophy/evidence boundary

A durable separation across the work is:

```text
Philosophy → motivates questions and lenses.
Methodology → determines how questions are investigated.
Evidence → records what happened.
Theory → explains evidence within bounded scope.
Engineering → turns supported structure into usable capability.
```

Philosophy may orient research; experiments cannot cite philosophy as evidence.

# II. General research and reconstruction frameworks

## R³ — Recursive Relational Reconstruction

**Status:** formal/methodological research line with verified finite and formal components; not scientific evidence for unrelated domains.

R³ developed around faithful reconstruction, recursive lifting, relation preservation, progressive repair, and typed part/representation/causal relations.

Recovered formal work includes grounded quantifier transport, anchored structural composition, adequacy-factorization kernels, persistent strict join-repair confluence, and rank-based termination. Countermodels showed that dropping repair persistence or valid-state join closure can break the stronger conclusions.

Operationally:

`distinguish → ground → transport → attack → repair → select`

## One_Level_Up / Airlock

**Status:** provenance-preserving transfer and admission framework.

Core obligations include origin, chronology, integrity state, provenance, interpretation separation, privacy minimization, admission control, symmetric ingress/egress, and historical-alias resolution.

A handoff carries evidence and lineage; it does not inherit authority merely because it came from an earlier project.

## Cross-Carrier Field / Transform Wave

**Status:** active research architecture with bounded experiments.

Focus: relations and transformations across different carriers while preserving task obligations and unresolved remainder.

Recurring questions:

- what exactly must a carrier preserve for the current task?
- what is lost in transformation?
- can the source be reconstructed?
- is the representation useful enough to justify its cost?
- is an apparent limitation a reality obstruction or a knowledge/representation obstruction?

## Obstruction distinction

Two broad obstruction families recur:

- **Reality obstruction Ω_R** — physical/structural impossibility, information-theoretic non-identifiability, irreversible loss, hard resource/environment limits, or genuine legal/rights/safety prohibition.
- **Knowledge obstruction Ω_K** — bad representation, routing/search failure, hidden dependency, measurement blindness, aliasing, provenance loss, translation loss, verifier coupling, interpretation error, wrong abstraction, or unpriced cost.

When the available experiment does not discriminate between them, the state remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## REDOGIT

**Status:** public working rule / engineering-research discipline.

`Do it. Inspect it. Redo it better.`

Preserve the predecessor and provenance, bound the change, build the smallest useful successor, verify the successor, retain failures/unresolved state, and continue without rewriting history.

# III. Knowledge, language, semantics, and memory

## Orbit / historical knowledge recovery

**Status:** active knowledge/recovery infrastructure.

Work includes active/history separation, source mapping, recovery indexes, typed lineage, variant graphs, UOID/content identity, cold locators, accessibility, and Knowledge Decay.

Central distinction:

`preserved != available != active`

A small active surface may be preferable when richer source/history layers remain recoverable.

## Knowledge Decay

**Status:** working knowledge/recovery problem class.

Knowledge can be lost even when bytes remain: relations, context, provenance, discoverability, accessibility, or the ability to reconstruct why something mattered may decay.

The recovery work therefore treats source, witness, edition, scan, transcription, translation, and reconstruction as distinct carriers.

## Tiny Babel

**Status:** exhaustively verified finite research lineage.

Tiny Babel studied a finite compositional subject, carrier replacement, exact routing/resolution, and compact representation. Exhaustive finite checking replaced example-only confidence.

The result is bounded: finite correctness does not imply universal language, global minimality, or open-ended semantic understanding.

## TBCL — Context Language Lab

**Status:** preserved native/runtime research with verified finite results.

TBCL carries portable semantics, explicit context records, strict evaluation, bounded native execution, active/preserved separation, and source/reconstruction boundaries.

One preserved TBCL 0.2 result independently reconstructed the complete frozen Tiny Babel query-to-selection/provenance relation with zero route and provenance errors. A weaker exact control was much slower in the recorded development measurement; this supports the compiled relation as an effort-reduction mechanism, not a global-minimality claim.

## CSOL / language quality

**Status:** working language-design formulation; empirical operationalization remains incomplete.

Working formulation:

`Language Quality = consequential meaning preserved / cognitive effort required`

Tracked qualities include:

- **Clarity:** readable, transparent, explicit, ambiguity-limited;
- **Consistency:** regular, predictable;
- **Economy**;
- **Composability**;
- **Reliability**.

Related principles include a simple human-facing interface and task-local definitions rather than unnecessary global ontology.

## Language Structure Workbench / Musilanguage / necessity-first language work

**Status:** separate experimental/design lines.

These explore inspectable language structure, unknown handling, current/audit separation, musical/procedural representation, and minimal-necessity interfaces. They are useful generators and interface research, not universal language theories.

# IV. Mathematics and formal computation

## Hodge conjecture / Fermat fourfold program

**Status:** substantial bounded mathematical reduction and explicit constructions; the general Hodge conjecture remains open.

The working reduction for Fermat fourfolds represents primitive Hodge blocks by character eigenspaces and seeks an algebraic cycle with nonzero projection into each target block.

Major mechanisms and results in the preserved line include:

- decomposable and quasi-decomposable Hodge blocks;
- residual blocks at selected degrees, with historical/reconstructed residual focus at `m = 33, 39, 45`;
- star-split / joining-line constructions through Fermat-curve factors;
- level-66 inflation and descent for the selected `m = 33` block;
- a Jacobi/Frobenius field-of-definition obstruction in which the relevant normalized eigenvalue has order 6, implying the residue-degree divisibility condition `6 | f` for a nonzero certifying projection in that setting;
- an explicit codimension-2 surface `Y ⊂ X_45^4` with the phase condition `c^9 = -5`;
- exact finite normalization arithmetic for the selected `m = 45` standard-character route;
- a remaining geometric intersection/Hodge-index kernel that must not be confused with the already-verified arithmetic normalization.

Critical boundary:

`field-of-definition obstruction != obstruction to algebraicity`

and

`finite character witness != completed geometric proof`

## Operator Moonshot

**Status:** active research laboratory with explicit claim ceilings.

Moonshot explores reusable operators, verified composition, semantic execution, search economics, education/calibration experiments, Hodge work, and controlled cross-domain transfers.

Recurring rule: replicate before promotion, preserve failed replications, use calibration only as calibration, and attack promising mechanisms with simpler baselines and counterexamples.

## P versus NP / SAT / decision-field research

**Status:** active bounded computation and negative-evidence program. `P ?= NP` remains open.

Research lines include SAT/Independent-Set exact solvers, selector audits, conditioning/planning cost, decision fields, exact carrier construction, boundary quotients, recursive decomposition, SEARCH/CERTIFY distinctions, polynomial/Chebyshev carriers, finite selector economics, and MLIR/LLVM representation work.

Important retained negative results include:

- automatic separator discovery was rejected as the default on one bounded graph panel because discovery cost usually exceeded direct solving;
- local exact carriers can dramatically reduce Boolean search while recursive boundary relations still grow with coupling scope;
- finite scaling panels do not establish unconditional lower bounds or asymptotic laws;
- Chebyshev/polynomial carrier experiments do not support generic search superiority.

A current consequential question is whether a large exact boundary relation can be replaced by a polynomial-size, polynomially discoverable exact carrier without merely re-encoding the original coupled CSP.

No bounded experiment is treated as a proof of `P = NP` or `P != NP`.

## Search / Certify / Recursive Decomposition

**Status:** bounded exact algorithmic research.

Distinguishes navigation/search from certification, explores separator discovery and boundary quotients, and recursively returns from local carrier compression to the next emergent boundary relation.

## Formal verification and solver work

**Status:** mixed formal/finite verification across projects.

Tools and techniques used across the body of work include Lean, Z3, cvc5, SMT-LIB, SymPy, exact finite enumeration, independent alternate verifiers, hash/integrity checking, witness reconstruction, and adversarial corruption tests.

# V. Computer mind, agents, and model systems

## Operational Closure Theory of Computer Mind

**Status:** proposed operational theory with a mixed experimental record; no consciousness/personhood claim.

The theory asks what smallest computational organization should count as a computer mind rather than merely a process. It proposes seven causally testable relations:

1. model–evidence loop;
2. self-indexing;
3. temporal integration;
4. counterfactual valuation;
5. integrated commitment;
6. consequence-based revision;
7. governed metacontrol.

Identity is modeled operationally through continuation state plus an identity kernel containing history/provenance, governance, authority, and lineage.

Experimental record includes:

- an original purpose-built toy in which all seven local probes passed within that toy;
- a more realistic TaskWorld expansion in which architecture-level performance and metacontrol gates failed;
- a valid negative Gate 1 result rejecting the tested EVSI metacontrol implementation;
- a later Gate 1B selection-plus-consumption component that passed its narrowly scoped frozen test;
- Gate 2 remained closed pending broader evidence.

Explicitly not established: phenomenal consciousness, qualia, personhood, moral status, biological equivalence, or general intelligence.

## SHADOW

**Status:** preserved model-control research line.

Focus: behavior-preserving control structures over frozen models, minimal executable intervention, prompt/control search, and regression-preserving experimentation without silently retraining the underlying model.

## PulseNet / PulseNet GP / SPDM / SLM / streaming memory / Micro-Ouro

**Status:** experimental model machinery with retained negative evidence.

These lines explore evolving update programs, curvature/sigma/trust/covariance mechanisms, raw-byte/tokenizer-free processing, associative/streaming memory, compressed-domain models, recurrence, mutation/search, and resource accounting.

Across the model work, execution correctness, numerical stability, causal mechanism activity, and predictive usefulness are kept separate. Several mechanisms changed outputs without earning usefulness or generality claims.

## Agents, societies, swarms, skills, and Conscience64 coordination

**Status:** implemented and proposed coordination machinery at different maturity levels.

The work distinguishes agents, skills, connectors, societies, swarms, role separation, authority, deterministic replay, evidence custody, promotion gates, and human purpose. Parallel activity does not automatically imply independent evidence or authority.

# X. Recurring theories, theorem-like results, and corollary patterns

This section groups reusable structures without pretending they all have theorem status.

## Theorem/formal-result class

Examples include:

- formally verified R³ kernels within their stated assumptions;
- exact finite Tiny Babel/TBCL equivalence and routing checks;
- explicit finite Hodge character calculations and selected descent/field-of-definition deductions;
- exact solver/certificate results in declared finite graph/SAT instances;
- exact encode/decode and identity results in declared carrier domains.

## Empirical-result class

Examples include:

- Computer Mind gated intervention results and retained failures;
- Cross-Carrier selector economics;
- recursive boundary-growth observations;
- model ablations and negative controls;
- practical engineering observations when actually run.

## Corollary-pattern class

Repeated consequences across many projects include:

1. **Correctness does not imply usefulness.**
2. **A carrier can be lossless for one obligation while destructive for another.**
3. **Integrity does not imply semantic truth.**
4. **A successful local decomposition does not imply global decomposition is cheap.**
5. **A representation may remove repeated effort without being globally minimal.**
6. **More coordination can increase capability without requiring homogenization.**
7. **A system can preserve identity through change only if the relevant continuity relations are preserved.**
8. **Unknown, absent, disproved, superseded, and unavailable are different states.**
9. **Discovery cost belongs in the evaluation of a discovered optimization.**
10. **Independent reconstruction is stronger evidence than trusting the producing system’s summary.**
11. **Human purpose, safety, rights, accessibility, and economic cost constrain what counts as a useful solution.**
12. **Cross-project resemblance is not evidence transfer.**

These are working cross-project principles unless a specific project supplies a formal proof.

# XII. What remains explicitly open

Among the most important unresolved boundaries:

- the general Hodge conjecture remains open;
- `P ?= NP` remains open;
- finite/implemented cross-carrier results do not establish a universal theory of semantics or knowledge;
- Operational Closure does not establish consciousness or personhood;
- broad transfer/generalization of many model mechanisms remains unestablished;
- exact historical recovery is incomplete where original carriers are missing;
- the best abstraction for recursive boundary relations in hard search remains unresolved;
- broad real-world benefit of some research frameworks has not been established outside bounded cases;
- the technical “we” is currently a structural/philosophical framework and research program, not a demonstrated universal law of organization.

# XIV. Governing rule for future updates

This map should grow **forward-only**.

When another older theory, theorem attempt, corollary, project, conversation, experiment, failure, or implementation is recovered:

1. preserve its original identity and chronology;
2. classify its evidence state;
3. link it to related work with a typed relation;
4. do not transfer authority across that relation;
5. record contradictions and negative results;
6. retain unresolved remainder;
7. revise this map without rewriting what earlier evidence actually showed.

The goal is not to make years of work look like one perfect theory.

The goal is to make the **whole recoverable body of work inspectable without losing the distinctions that make it trustworthy**.
